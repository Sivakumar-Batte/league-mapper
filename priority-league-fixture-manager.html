                          Creation Workbench

                          The workbench is derived automatically from the seven-day collection, your active priority mappings, and your worked-fixture review decisions — it is the set of priority Flashscore fixtures that are not confirmed already-worked. It recomputes on its own when any of those inputs change; there is no rebuild button. The original seven Flashscore columns (Sport, Date, Time, Country, League, Home, Away) are preserved verbatim; Home and Away additionally show the mapped BCM name in brackets. Betradar never affects which fixtures appear here (it is comparison-only).

                          Flashscore fixtures

                          Process one sport and one Flashscore date at a time, then add it to the collection. A collection entry is identified by sport + date: adding the same sport and date again replaces only that entry, and other sports on the same date are untouched. The collection holds up to 7 distinct calendar dates (all sports on one date count as one day); once seven dates are collected, adding an eighth distinct date is blocked until you remove an older date. Clear empties the whole collection. The Flashscore dataset used for comparison is the union of every collected entry. Saved in this browser's Local Storage. Nothing is sent to BCM.
